Convoy — Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

Convoy is a beta app for riding groups: it shows everyone in a ride on a shared live map. That only works by sharing your location with the people you ride with, so this page explains plainly what is collected, who sees it, and where it goes.

What Convoy collects

There are no accounts, emails or passwords. When you first open the app it creates an anonymous identity for you; nothing links it to who you are beyond the name you type.

  • Your name, exactly as you type it when you create or join a ride.
  • Your live location and speed, while you are in a live ride. It is sent every few seconds while you move, and periodically while you are stopped. On Android this carries on with the screen locked or another app open, and stops when the ride ends or you leave it.
  • Your phone's battery level and whether power saving is on, read on the phone only to decide how often to check your location. They are not sent anywhere.
  • Ride details: the route, destination, ride name, and who the leader is.
  • Reports made during a ride: pitstop reports, regroup points and SOS alerts, each with your name and the position where you made them.
  • A record of finished rides: ride name, distance, duration, route, and the names of the riders who were on it.
  • A push notification token, only if you allow notifications, so the group can reach you.
  • Live location links, only if you choose to make one: a random link tied to you and the ride, and whether it is still active. The page it opens shows your name, live position, speed, the ride's name, destination and route.
  • Bug reports and error reports: what you typed, or the error, plus your app version, phone model, Android or iOS version, and the ride you were in.
  • Your phone's location is also used once when you create a ride, to suggest your starting point.

Who can see it

Everyone in a ride can see the name and live location of everyone else in that ride, plus the reports made during it. That is the purpose of the app.

Anyone who knows a ride's code can look up its basic details (name and route) and join it, so treat the code like a key and share it only with the people you intend to ride with. People who are not in your ride cannot see your live location.

Convoy's developer can see the stored data through the database dashboard, including bug reports, in order to run and fix the app.

Live location links (optional)

You can share your own live position with someone who is not in the app, by sending them a link. Anyone who has the link can open it and see your name, where you are, how fast you are going, and the ride's name, destination and route. It never shows the other riders in your ride.

A link stops working when the ride ends, when you stop sharing or leave the ride, or after 24 hours, whichever comes first. While one is active, a SHARING label stays visible on your ride screen, and tapping it stops the link straight away. Treat a link like a key: anyone you forward it to can follow you until it stops.

Services that receive data

Convoy relies on outside services to work. Each of them receives the following.

  • Supabase hosts the database, anonymous sign-in and live updates. Everything listed above is stored there.
  • Expo delivers push notifications (your token and the message text) and app updates (your app version and device type).
  • OpenFreeMap supplies the map tiles. Like any map, it can tell roughly which area is being viewed, and it sees your network address.
  • Photon (by Komoot) powers place search when you plan a ride: it receives what you type, and the coordinates when you drop a pin or use your location.
  • Open-Meteo supplies the weather shown for a ride's destination. It receives the destination's coordinates only, with no name or other identifier.
  • If you share a live location link, the web page it opens is served by a separate static host and loads its map from OpenFreeMap. Visitors' network addresses are visible to those services in the usual way.
  • The public OSRM routing service calculates routes and snaps GPS points to roads. It receives the start and end of a planned route, and the recent GPS path of riders who have left the planned route.

How long it is kept

Your live position is removed when you leave a ride or the leader ends it, and lobby entries are removed when the ride starts. If your phone dies mid-ride, a position that has not updated for 12 hours is removed automatically.

A ride nobody started is removed after 24 hours. Once a ride has ended, its details, its pitstop and SOS reports and any live location links are removed after 7 days. Bug and error reports are removed after 90 days. The record of a finished ride in your ride history (ride name, distance, duration, route and the names of the riders) is kept for 365 days.

You do not have to wait for any of that: Settings, Delete my data removes everything listed here at once.

Your choices

  • Location is needed for a ride to work. You can turn it off in your phone's settings at any time, but live tracking will stop.
  • Notifications are optional. Declining them only means you will not be alerted while the app is closed.
  • The camera is used only to scan a ride's QR code. Nothing from the camera is stored or sent.
  • You can leave a ride at any time, which stops sharing your position immediately.
  • You can delete your data at any time from Settings, Delete my data. It removes your name, the rides you created, your ride history entries, your reports and your anonymous identity from Convoy's servers, and resets the app on your phone. In finished-ride records that other riders share, you are taken out and your name is replaced with "A former rider". If you are leading a ride that other riders are still in, end it or hand it over first.

Location and background use

Convoy only tracks your location while you are in a live ride, and never when you are not. During a ride, tracking continues when you lock your phone or switch to another app, so the group can still see you. On Android, that is done with a foreground service, and a "Convoy ride in progress" notification stays visible for as long as it is running.

Tracking stops as soon as you leave the ride, the ride ends, or you close the app. Convoy does not ask for the "Allow all the time" location permission.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and the database is set up so that only riders in a ride can read that ride's locations and reports. This is a beta app, so please do not treat it as a hardened system.

Children

Convoy is not aimed at children, and it is not designed to collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: use Settings, then Report a problem, inside the app.
